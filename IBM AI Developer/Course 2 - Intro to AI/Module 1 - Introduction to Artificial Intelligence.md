#typesOfAI

![[Pasted image 20260925001802.png]]

*Weak AI (narrow AI)*
- An AI tailored for specific domains
- Decision-making based on programmed algorithms and training data
Example: Language translators, virtual assistants, AI-powered web searches, recommendation engines, Intelligent spam filters.

*Strong AI (generalized AI*
- An AI with diverse capabilities across unrelated tasks
- Acquires new skills to face new challenges
- Is an amalgamation of various AI strategies
Examples: Finance, human resources, information technology, Research and Development, supply chain.

*Super AI (Conscious AI)*
- Ai with human-level consciousness
- Requires self-awareness
Examples: Healthcare, autonomous vehicles, robotics, natural language understanding, environmental conservation. 

## Artificial Intelligence vs. Augmented Intelligence


Artificial Intelligence: ability  for machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligences, such as reasoning, natural communication, and problem solving.
Augmented Intelligence: Augmented intelligence systems augment out human abilities. Examples: screen readers fo the blind, voice-driven navigation.
