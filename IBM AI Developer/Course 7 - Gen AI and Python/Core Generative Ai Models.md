Foundation Models

Center for research on Foundation Models: 

A new successful parading for building AI systems. Train one model on a huge amount of data and adapt it to many applications. We call such a model a foundation model. 
**Large, general-purpose Self-supervised Pre-trained on unlabeled data, billions of parameters**

![[Pasted image 20261122143157.png]]

Large versus limited models 

| Foundation models            | Smaller GEN AI models             |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Broad skillset               | Trained on restricted domain data |
| Relevant to multiple domains | Perform limited tasks             |
| Ex. Dall-E                   | AlexNet                           |

>[!tip] Foundation Models
>All foundation models have generative AI capabilities 
>Not all generative AI models are foundation models
>
