*Views*

- Present data from one or more tables or additional views. Enables interaction with data as with tables 
- Simplifies data retrieval and reduces access to underlying tables. 

*Materialized view*
- Behave differently  to regular view
- Result set is materialized, or saved, for future use 
- Cannot insert, update, or delete rows
