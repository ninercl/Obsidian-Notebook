- **Entities** represent people, objects, or concepts. Serve as a fundamental building block. 
- **Relationship** sets illustrate how entities are interconnected. They represent connections between different entities. 
- **Crow's foot** notations indicates relationship between entities. Employs symbols to signify nature and quantity.
